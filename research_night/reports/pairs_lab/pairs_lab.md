# Pairs lab

Classic pairs trading inside the k-means peer clusters, pairs re-formed every January on the past year's correlations (> 0.6), z-score of the log-price spread over `window` days, enter when |z| > `entry`, exit when |z| < `exit` or after `max_hold` days. Unit capital split equally across pairs; net of measured spreads + 1 bp on every leg. Sharpe is of the raw (unlevered) book; vol is ~1-2%, so it would be levered in a real book.
