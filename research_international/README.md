# research_international

This is the international extension of the momentum-with-a-stress-switch
work:
- 41 exchanges (21 index universes plus 20 exchanges that were held out
  and tested against pre-registered predictions);
- every listed Asia-Pacific financial (1,341 names, by industry).

It is a separate model. It does not change Arjun's code or `research_night/`.

- `REPORT.md`: findings, the numbers to plan with, what not to trade.
- `LOG.md`: the running log, in order, including the failed tests.
- `headline_numbers.csv`: the key Sharpe ratios in one table.
- `code/`: everything needed to rebuild the data and the results
  (commands at the end of `REPORT.md`).
- `reports/`: result tables (CSV). Daily series and prices are not
  committed.
  - `reports/universes/`: the three universe lists.

Data comes from Yahoo, Wikipedia and FRED. Nothing here needs a key.
